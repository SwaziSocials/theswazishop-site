Our story

Health, unity and abundance

The Swazi Shop is a Black-owned holistic brand making earth-centered products for people of all walks of life who want fewer chemicals and a closer relationship with nature.

Today's Swazi Shop is a revival of the 1950s original: the first Pan-African store in East Chicago, opened by siblings of the Dawkins family to share their history and celebrate cultural pride.
